2Phishy: Pre-test Assessment
Good Day!

We are third-year BS Information Technology students from Mapúa University – Makati Campus conducting our thesis entitled "2Phishy: A Gamified Learning Application for Cybersecurity Awareness."
Thank you for participating in this pre-playtest assessment. This questionnaire aims to measure your current cybersecurity awareness, experiences with online scams, and ability to recognize common scam scenarios before using the 2Phishy application. Your responses will serve as the baseline for evaluating the effectiveness of our gamified cybersecurity learning application.

This assessment consists of questions about your demographic information, cybersecurity awareness, prior experiences with online scams, and scenario-based cybersecurity situations. Please answer each question honestly based on your own knowledge and experience.

Your participation is voluntary, and all responses will be kept strictly confidential. The information collected will be used solely for academic research purposes.

In accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), all data collected will be securely stored, treated with confidentiality, and permanently deleted after the completion of the study. Only the authorized research team will have access to the collected information.

As a participant, you have the right to:
Keep your identity and responses confidential.
Withdraw from the study at any time without penalty.
Request the removal of your personal data from the study by contacting the researchers.
For further questions, you may reach us through the following:
Jahnreil J. Amarillento - jjamarillento@mymail.mapua.edu.ph
Mon Andre V. Perez - mavperez@mymail.mapua.edu.ph

2Phishy: Post-test Assessment

Good Day!

We are third-year BS Information Technology students from Mapúa University – Makati Campus conducting our thesis entitled "2Phishy: A Gamified Learning Application for Cybersecurity Awareness."

Thank you for participating in the 2Phishy playtest. This post-playtest questionnaire aims to evaluate your cybersecurity knowledge, awareness, and learning experience after using the 2Phishy application. Your responses will be compared with your pre-test responses to assess the effectiveness of the application as a gamified cybersecurity learning tool.

This questionnaire consists of questions about your cybersecurity knowledge, confidence in recognizing cybersecurity threats, and your experience using the application. Please answer each question honestly based on your current knowledge and your experience while using 2Phishy.

Your participation remains voluntary, and all responses will be treated with strict confidentiality. The information collected will be used solely for academic research purposes.

In accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), all collected information will be securely stored, kept confidential, and permanently deleted upon the completion of the study. Your responses will be matched with your pre-test questionnaire using your assigned Participant ID while preserving your anonymity.

As a participant, you have the right to:

Keep your identity and responses confidential.
Withdraw from the study at any time without penalty.
Request the removal of your data from the study by contacting the researchers.

For further questions regarding this study, you may contact:
